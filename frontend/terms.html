Terms and privacy consent

Personal Data Processing Consent

By creating an account in Langly and selecting the checkbox on the sign-up page, you confirm that you have read this notice and agree to the processing of your personal data for the purposes described below.

What data may be processed

Langly may process the information you provide during registration and use of the application, including your email address, selected native language, selected study language, folders, modules, saved words, notes, learning progress, quiz results, and technical information needed to keep your session active.

Why the data is processed

Your data is used to create and maintain your account, personalize your learning experience, save vocabulary and notes, track study progress, provide flashcards, quizzes and lessons, protect your account, and improve the reliability of the app.

Your consent

Consent is given by a clear action: ticking the checkbox before creating an account. You should only create an account if you understand and agree that Langly will process your personal data for the purposes listed on this page.

Your rights

You may request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion of your account data, or withdrawal of your consent. Withdrawing consent may limit or prevent continued use of features that require an account.

Data storage

Langly stores account and learning data only for as long as it is needed to provide the application features or until you request deletion, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable law.
